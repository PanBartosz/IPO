# IPO Warsaw 2026 — Registration & Surveys Guide

This guide covers the **launch-phase** workflows in the IPO Warsaw 2026 portal:

- delegation leader registration (**by invitation from the organizers**)
- inviting students and teachers (**by the delegation leader**)
- registration for invited participants (students and teachers)
- completing organizer surveys (students and teachers)

> **Delegation leaders:** after you register, your key responsibility in the portal is to **invite all members of your delegation** (students and teachers). Each invited person must then **complete their own registration** using the personal link sent by email (or shared by the delegation leader).

This guide intentionally **does not** cover essay upload or grading. Those instructions will be published later.

## 1. Portal home (invite-only)

When registrations are invite-only, the portal home shows an information banner and the navigation menu.

![Portal home (invite-only)](assets/home.png)

## 2. Delegation leader registration (invite from the organizers)

Delegation leaders register through a **personal invitation link** sent by the organizers (via email).

1. Open the invitation link.
2. Confirm the prefilled **email** and **country** (they are locked to the invite).
3. Fill in the remaining required fields and consents.
4. Click **Submit registration**.

![Delegation leader (teacher) registration — form](assets/invite-teacher-open.png)

After you fill the form, submit it:

![Delegation leader (teacher) registration — filled](assets/invite-teacher-filled.png)

After submitting, you should see a success message. You will receive your **Teacher Portal credentials** by email.

![Leader registration submitted](assets/leader-registration-submitted.png)

> The same **Teacher registration** form is used for teachers invited by delegation leaders (the only difference is who sends the invitation).

## 3. Teacher Portal — sign in (delegation leader)

Open the Teacher Portal login page and enter the **username** and **password** you received by email from the organizers.

![Teacher Portal login](assets/teacher-login.png)

After signing in, delegation leaders have access to **Delegation invites** and **Surveys** from the dashboard.

![Teacher Portal dashboard](assets/teacher-dashboard.png)

## 4. Invite participants (students and teachers)

Delegation leaders invite students and teachers from the Teacher Portal. Each invite triggers an **automatic email** to the participant with a personal registration link.

1. Open **Delegation invites** from the dashboard.
2. Create invites for students and teachers.
3. If needed, you can also copy the **invite link** from the table (for example, to resend it manually).

![Delegation invites](assets/delegation-invites.png)

### 4.1 Create a student invite

1. Fill in the student’s **first name**, **last name**, and **email**.
2. Click **Send student invite**.
3. Copy the invite link (if needed) and send it to the student.

![Student invite created](assets/delegation-invites-student-created.png)

### 4.2 Create a teacher invite

1. Fill in the teacher’s **first name**, **last name**, and **email**.
2. Click **Send teacher invite**.
3. Copy the invite link (if needed) and send it to the teacher.

![Teacher invite created](assets/delegation-invites-teacher-created.png)

## 5. Participant registration (invite link)

Participants register through an **invite link** shared by the delegation leader. The portal will show the correct form based on the invitation type:

- **Student registration** (students)
- **Teacher registration** (teachers)

After registration is complete, the participant should see a success message and will receive their portal credentials by email. If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder and then contact the organizers.

### 5.1 Student registration

Click **Open invite** (or paste the link in a browser) to open the student registration form.

![Student invite — registration form](assets/invite-student-open.png)

On the registration form:

1. Confirm prefilled **email** and **country** (they are locked to the invite).
2. Fill in **school**.
3. Select accommodation options.
4. Enter **date of birth**.
5. Choose preferences (language, keyboard, meals).
6. Complete required consents.
7. Click **Submit registration**.

![Student registration form filled](assets/invite-student-filled.png)

After submitting, you should see a success message.

![Student registration submitted](assets/student-registration-submitted.png)

### 5.2 Teacher registration

Teacher registration uses the same flow shown in **Section 2** (Teacher registration form + success message).

## 6. Surveys (students and teachers)

Surveys are created and assigned by the organizers. When a new survey is published, you will typically receive an email notification and you will also see it in your portal.

### 6.1 Teachers (delegation leaders and invited teachers)

From the Teacher Portal dashboard, open the surveys list.

![Teacher portal dashboard (surveys access)](assets/teacher-dashboard.png)

On the surveys page:

1. Review the survey status summary.
2. In **Outstanding surveys**, click **Complete survey**.

![Teacher surveys list](assets/teacher-surveys-list.png)

Fill in the answers and click **Submit response**.

![Teacher survey detail](assets/teacher-survey-detail.png)

After submitting, you should see confirmation. If the survey allows edits, you may see **Update response**.

![Teacher survey submitted](assets/teacher-survey-submitted.png)

### 6.2 Students

From the Participant Portal dashboard, open the surveys list.

![Student portal dashboard (surveys access)](assets/student-portal.png)

On the surveys page:

1. In **Outstanding surveys**, click **Complete survey**.

![Student surveys list](assets/student-surveys-list.png)

Fill in the answers and click **Submit response**.

![Student survey detail](assets/student-survey-detail.png)

After submitting, you should see confirmation. If the survey allows edits, you may see **Update response**.

![Student survey submitted](assets/student-survey-submitted.png)
